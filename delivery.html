The Kybern Delivery Framework

Five stages from investment case to handover.

A hybrid approach that applies PMI-style rigour to capital and transformation work and Agile to product work, inside a portfolio office structured on P3O.

STAGE 1

Align

Link strategy to objectives and key results. Set the investment case and success measures at kick-off.

OutputInvestment case, OKRs
STAGE 2

Structure

Stand up the portfolio office, roles, gates and decision rights. Baseline maturity with P3M3.

OutputGovernance model, maturity baseline
STAGE 3

Deliver

Run the right method for each work type. Delivery leads own cost, schedule and margin targets from kick-off.

OutputWorking releases, tracked targets
STAGE 4

Prove

One portfolio view, updated monthly. Benefits tracked against the case, with executive and board reporting.

OutputPortfolio dashboard, benefits report
STAGE 5

Hand over

Documented handover and capability transfer. Move to managed service where it makes sense.

OutputHandover pack, run model
